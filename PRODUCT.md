# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Villa Azuréa serves high-net-worth buyers, brokers, and real-estate professionals evaluating a private estate in Mougins on the Côte d'Azur. They arrive through a qualified introduction, listing context, or private viewing conversation, and need to quickly understand the property's scale, setting, privacy, and emotional character.

## Product Purpose

The site is a single-page bilingual marketing experience for Villa Azuréa. It exists to communicate the estate's character and arrange private viewings, not to capture leads at scale. Success means the property feels rare, culturally rooted, and appointment-worthy before a visitor ever speaks to an agent.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic Riviera, quiet confidence, cultured privacy. The experience should feel atmospheric and transportive, led by landscape, light, architecture, and restraint rather than overt sales language.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic luxury brokerage templates, mass lead-generation funnels, overfilled amenity grids, glossy real-estate cliches, and SaaS-style landing page patterns. The site should not feel like a portal, a dashboard, a brochure dumped onto the web, or an interchangeable "premium villa" listing.

## Design Principles

1. Let the place persuade first. Video, photography, spatial pacing, and the Mougins setting should carry more weight than claims.
2. Practice selective disclosure. Reveal only the details that deepen desire or confidence, then guide qualified visitors toward a private viewing.
3. Keep the voice cinematic, not loud. Luxury comes from tempo, composition, and specificity, not decorative excess.
4. Preserve bilingual polish. English and French copy should feel native, restrained, and equally complete.
5. Protect the estate's privacy. Contact paths should feel curated and appointment-based rather than transactional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Preserve strong contrast, meaningful alt text, keyboard access for navigation and gallery interactions, explicit focus states, semantic structure, and reduced-motion alternatives for scroll, reveal, parallax, video, and carousel motion.
