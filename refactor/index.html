The Estate

A 600 m² oasis
above the Bay of Cannes.

Located in Mougins, in the heart of an exclusive private gated community of ten villas including the historic Château de Lérins, Villa Azuréa embodies the French Riviera art of living combined with modern luxury.

A rare oasis of peace overlooking the Bay of Cannes, the estate is set on 2.1 hectares of landscaped grounds and offers an exceptional 300-degree panoramic view of the sea and surrounding mountains.

Designed as a complex of three buildings — main house, guest house and pool house — the property combines turnkey contemporary interiors with the warmth and craftsmanship of the Provençal tradition.

The Estate

In the hills of Mougins — a village that drew Picasso, who made it his home, and welcomed Cocteau, Léger, and Man Ray before him — Villa Azuréa rests on 2.1 hectares of cypress, olive, and wild rosemary above the Bay of Cannes.

Six hundred square metres of living space open to a seventeen-metre infinity pool that meets the horizon. The sea on one side, the Préalpes on the other.

Indoor · Outdoor

Where the horizon
becomes the room.

Floor-to-ceiling glazing dissolves the line between living spaces and the Mediterranean panorama. From the covered terrace to the infinity edge of the pool, the estate is designed for the choreography of sun, sea and slow afternoons.

Location

In the hills
of Mougins.

Set in the historic perched village of Mougins — long beloved by Picasso and Cocteau — the villa is moments from Cannes, twenty-five minutes from Nice Côte d'Azur Airport, and within easy reach of Antibes, Monaco and the perfumeries of Grasse.
